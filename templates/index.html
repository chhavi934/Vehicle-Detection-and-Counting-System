Vehicle Detection and Counting

This system detects, tracks, and counts vehicles in video footage using computer vision techniques.

Features:
  • Detect vehicles in video footage or camera streams
  • Track vehicles across frames
  • Count vehicles passing through defined areas
  • Classify different vehicle types (car, truck, motorcycle, bus)
  • Generate statistics and export to CSV
